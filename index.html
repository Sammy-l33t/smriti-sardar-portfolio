04 / Endurance & Athletics

Every Mile Tells a Story

Marathon running is my discipline engine. When your legs burn at kilometer 35, you don't negotiate with discomfort — you keep your pace. That same grit guides every late-night editorial cutdown and high-altitude summit pitch.

The Backyard Ultra Mindset

In collaboration with Bigfoot Adventure India (BFAI), I directed and edited the narrative sports documentary “One Last Loop”, demystifying the world’s most relentless endurance running format: running 4.167 miles every single hour on the hour until only one person remains standing.

Endurance running reshaped my creative philosophy. It stripped away impatience, taught me the power of micro-rhythms, and instilled a quiet resilience that keeps me calm during high-pressure film shoots and tight festival deadlines.

Discipline over Motivation

Motivation is fleeting. Discipline is waking up before dawn, lacing up trail shoes in sub-zero wind, and doing the work whether you feel inspired or not.
